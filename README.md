# SpaceEngine Manual APWorld
This is a simple Archipelago Manual for SpaceEngine, a simulator for exploring space at 1:1 scale. It consists of receiving new speeds (velocities) as a progressive item and using it to visit planets, stars, nebulae, galaxies, black holes, and quasars.

You aren't allowed to use any form of teleportation or instant traveling, meaning you will be manually flying to everything (you may use it to fast travel to places you've already visited). You can use selection pointers and search for objects of course so you know where to go. I've also done my best to ensure logic makes travel times reasonable (around a minute or two at longest for a given destination).

You start at Earth, and the final goal is the black hole TON 618, one of the farthest things from Earth in SpaceEngine.

You may go slower but not faster than the most recent speed you've received. Use the in-game next/previous velocity keybinds to switch between these speed intervals more easily.

Note that after you've unlocked 100.00 Mly/s (maximum speed), you can squeeze out about triple that speed by using "increase velocity" (it's a different command than "next velocity").

Also note that Progressive Velocities 4, 5, and 6 don't offer any checks simply because of the sheer gap between our solar system and the next nearest star.

5. 100.00 AU/sec
6. 1000.00 AU/sec
7. 0.10 ly/sec
8. 1.00 ly/sec

12. 10.00 kly/sec
13. 100.00 kly/sec
